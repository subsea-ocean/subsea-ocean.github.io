<p style="

  line-height:1.8;
">

Benedetto Barone is a co-PI of the SUBSEA project investigating the ecological and biogeochemical dynamics of the open ocean with a focus on phytoplankton photosynthesis and particle export. He is interested in characterizing the physical-biological dynamics associated with turbulent mixing and mesoscale eddies.

</p>

<p style="
  font-size:1.05em;
  line-height:1.8;
">

He uses autonomous vehicles to collect underwater measurements including bio-optical proxies for the ecological characteristics of marine environments.

</p>

<p style="
  font-size:1.05em;
  line-height:1.8;
">

He received his PhD in Marine Sciences and Engineering in Naples, Italy, from the Università “Federico II” and the Stazione Zoologica Anton Dohrn. Previously, he received an MSc in Environmental Sciences in Naples from the Università “Parthenope”.

</p>

<p style="
  font-size:1.05em;
  line-height:1.8;
">

He is currently an ocean researcher at the University of Hawaiʻi.

</p>
