Katie Coates Project Manager

University of Montana, USA

Katie Coates is an early-career biological scientist and recent graduate of the University of Montana, where she earned her Bachelor of Science degree in May 2025. She is a Research Associate in the Church Lab at the University of Montana’s Flathead Lake Biological Station and serves as Project Manager for the SUBSEA project. Her work combines project coordination with field and laboratory research in marine microbial ecology and ocean biogeochemistry.
