<p style="

  line-height:1.8;
">

Pierre Marrec is a Marine Research Associate at the Graduate School of Oceanography, University of Rhode Island, with expertise in ocean biogeochemistry and plankton ecology.

</p>

<p style="
  font-size:1.05em;
  line-height:1.8;
">

His research is primarily observation-based, and he has spent significant time at sea aboard research vessels.

</p>

<p style="
  font-size:1.05em;
  line-height:1.8;
">

Marrec joined the OMI project in October 2024 as a part-time data manager and field coordinator. He was recently selected as a part-time Senior Field Coordinator for OneOBVI, where he is responsible for overseeing all core measurements collected during OBVI cruises aboard the <i>Falkor</i> (too) over the next three years.

</p>

<p style="
  font-size:1.05em;
  line-height:1.8;
">

He also leads underway observations during these expeditions. Passionate about all types of oceanographic data — especially field data — Marrec is committed to sharing Core Measurements results with the OBVI community and collaborating closely with project partners.

</p>
