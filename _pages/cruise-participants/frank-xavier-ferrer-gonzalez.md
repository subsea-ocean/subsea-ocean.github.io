<p style="

  line-height:1.8;
">

I’m fascinated by the ocean and the microscopic world that shapes most of the processes defining life as we know it, even though we cannot see it with our eyes.

</p>

<p style="
  font-size:1.05em;
  line-height:1.8;
">

My research focuses on microbial interactions in the surface ocean, particularly metabolite exchange between phytoplankton and heterotrophic bacteria. I am interested in how these interactions influence global biogeochemical cycles and how we can better understand them in the context of environmental change.

</p>

<p style="
  font-size:1.05em;
  line-height:1.8;
">

My approach to science is shaped by a commitment to equity, accountability, and systemic change. I am interested in how research can serve communities rather than extract from them, and I work to challenge the colonial structures that still define much of academic science.

</p>

<p style="
  font-size:1.05em;
  line-height:1.8;
">

I believe microbes hold solutions to some of the most urgent challenges of the Anthropocene, but only if we approach this work with care, collaboration, and critical reflection.

</p>

<p style="
  font-size:1.05em;
  line-height:1.8;
">

I also strive to create spaces in science where everyone has the opportunity to thrive. I value mentorship, transparency, and building environments that support curiosity without gatekeeping.

</p>
