<p style="font-size:1.05em; line-height:1.8;">
Calvin Swart is an ocean scientist whose research focuses on marine biogeochemistry and ecosystem processes in the open ocean.
</p>

<p style="font-size:1.05em; line-height:1.8;">
His work involves oceanographic field sampling, biogeochemical observations, and studies of plankton dynamics and nutrient cycling across marine ecosystems.
</p>

<p style="font-size:1.05em; line-height:1.8;">
As part of SUBSEA and Hawaiʻi Ocean Time-series cruises, he contributes to collaborative efforts investigating the ecological and biogeochemical functioning of the North Pacific subtropical gyre.
