<p style="font-size:1.05em; line-height:1.8;">
Hilary Close is an oceanographer whose research focuses on marine biogeochemistry and the cycling of carbon and nitrogen in the ocean.
</p>

<p style="font-size:1.05em; line-height:1.8;">
Her work combines chemical measurements, isotope approaches, and oceanographic field observations to investigate how biological and physical processes shape marine nutrient dynamics.
</p>

<p style="font-size:1.05em; line-height:1.8;">
Through participation in SUBSEA and Hawaiʻi Ocean Time-series cruises, she contributes to studies examining biogeochemical variability and ecosystem functioning in the North Pacific subtropical gyre.
</p>
