<h1 style="text-align:center; margin-top:10px;">
  HOT Cruise 358: Expedition to Station ALOHA
</h1>

<p style="text-align:center; font-size:1.15em; margin-top:-10px;">
  September 5–11, 2025
</p>

<p>
  The SUBSEA team has been collecting samples on cruises in the North Pacific
  subtropical gyre as part of the
  <a href="https://hahana.soest.hawaii.edu/hot/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">
    Hawaii Ocean Time Series
  </a>,
  which is managed by the School of Ocean and Earth Science and Technology
  at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa.
</p>
