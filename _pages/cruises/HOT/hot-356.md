<h1 style="text-align:center; margin-top:10px;">
  HOT Cruise 356: Expedition to Station ALOHA
</h1>

<p style="text-align:center; font-size:1.15em; margin-top:-10px;">
  February 10–14, 2025
</p>

The SUBSEA team has been collecting samples on cruises in the North Pacific subtropical gyre as part of the Hawaii Ocean Time Series, which is managed by the School of Ocean and Earth Science and Technology at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa.
