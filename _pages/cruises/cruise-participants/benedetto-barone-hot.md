<p style="font-size:1.05em; line-height:1.8;">
Benedetto Barone is an oceanographer whose research focuses on marine biogeochemistry, microbial ecology, and the role of plankton in global nutrient cycling.
</p>

<p style="font-size:1.05em; line-height:1.8;">
His work combines field observations, oceanographic measurements, and ecosystem-scale analyses to better understand how biological processes influence carbon and nutrient fluxes in the open ocean.
</p>

<p style="font-size:1.05em; line-height:1.8;">
As part of SUBSEA and the Hawaiʻi Ocean Time-series program, he contributes to studies investigating plankton productivity, biogeochemical variability, and ecosystem functioning in the North Pacific subtropical gyre.
</p>
