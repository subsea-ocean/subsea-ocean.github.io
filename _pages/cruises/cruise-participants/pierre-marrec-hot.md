<p style="font-size:1.05em; line-height:1.8;">
Pierre Marrec is a Marine Research Associate at the Graduate School of Oceanography at the University of Rhode Island with expertise in ocean biogeochemistry and plankton ecology.
</p>

<p style="font-size:1.05em; line-height:1.8;">
His research is primarily observation-based, and he has spent significant time at sea aboard research vessels conducting oceanographic fieldwork and coordinating scientific operations.
</p>

<p style="font-size:1.05em; line-height:1.8;">
As part of OneOBVI and SUBSEA, Pierre oversees core measurements collected during cruises aboard the R/V Falkor (too) and contributes to underway observations and field coordination across oceanographic expeditions.
</p>
