Several members of the SUBSEA team recently joined the Ocean Biogeochemical Virtual Institute annual meeting in Cape Town, South Africa. The meeting provided an opportunity to showcase SUBSEA science and to connect with the broader OBVI network. The valuable in-person time was spent on informational sessions about South Atlantic Ocean biogeochemistry, continuing to plan for upcoming cruises, and establishing new collaborations with OBVI scientists. And the food and drink was fabulous!
