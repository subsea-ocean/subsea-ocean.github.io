## Cruise updates 

Members of the SUBSEA team participated in the monthly [Hawai'i Ocean Time-series (HOT)](https://hahana.soest.hawaii.edu/hot/) research cruise in September, collecting samples and running experiments that will contribute to our understanding of the biogeochemical functioning of the North Pacific Subtropical Gyre. The cruise visited [Station ALOHA](https://hahana.soest.hawaii.edu/stationaloha/), about 100 km north of Oahu. See photos below!
